How to use it

The first calculator counts how many days separate two dates. By default the start date is not counted and the end date is, which matches how most people count “days until” something; tick “Include the end date” to count both. Tick “Weekdays only” to skip Saturdays and Sundays, which is useful for business deadlines and notice periods. Public holidays are not removed, because they vary by country and region.

The second calculator moves a date forward or back. Days and weeks are exact. Months and years follow the calendar: if the target month does not have that day, the result lands on the month’s last day, so January 31 plus one month is February 28 (or 29 in a leap year). Calculations use calendar dates only, so daylight saving clock changes never shift the count by a day.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later one. The calculator does this exactly, including leap days such as February 29, 2028.

Does it count the start and end dates?

It counts the days from the start up to the end, so the start date is excluded. Tick “Include the end date” to add one day and count both.

Are holidays excluded in weekdays-only mode?

No. Only Saturdays and Sundays are skipped. Subtract any public holidays that fall in your range yourself.

What is January 31 plus one month?

February 28, or February 29 in a leap year. When a month is too short, the result moves to its last day.
