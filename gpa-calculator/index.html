GPA Calculator

Work out your semester and cumulative GPA on the standard US 4.0 scale from letter grades and credit hours.

Include previous semesters (cumulative GPA)

How to use it

List each course with its credit hours and the letter grade you earned or expect. Your GPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points: each grade’s points are multiplied by the course credits, added up, and divided by the total credits. To see your cumulative GPA, open “Include previous semesters” and enter your current GPA and the credits it covers.

This calculator uses the common unweighted 4.0 scale below, where A+ counts the same as A. Some schools give 4.33 for A+ or add points for honors and AP classes, so check your school’s scale. Your courses are saved in this browser so you can come back and update them.

Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

Multiply each course’s grade points by its credits, add the results, and divide by total credits.

Does an A+ count as 4.3?

Not on this calculator. Many US colleges cap A+ at 4.0; check your school’s policy.

Is my data saved?

Your course list is stored in your browser’s local storage on this device only.
