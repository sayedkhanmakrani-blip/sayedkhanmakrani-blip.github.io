How to use it

Choose the time zone you are converting from, then the date and time. The table shows the same moment in every city on your list, with the date, a “next day” or “previous day” tag when the date changes, and the UTC offset on that date. Add cities with the selector below the table, reorder them with the arrow, or remove them with ✕. Your list and 12-hour/24-hour choice are remembered on this device.

The meeting planner lays out all 24 hours of the chosen day side by side. Green cells are regular office hours (9 am to 5 pm) in each place, pale cells are early morning or evening, and grey cells are night, so you can spot a time that suits everyone. Click any column to convert that hour. Daylight saving changes are applied automatically for the date you pick.

To announce an event across time zones, set the time and cities, then press Copy link under “Share this time” and paste it into an email or chat. Opening the link shows the same moment in every listed city.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your browser’s built-in time zone database, which applies daylight saving rules for the date you pick.

Is my list of cities saved?

Yes, in your browser’s local storage on this device only. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I share a converted time?

Use “Share this time” below the results. The link stores the date, time, starting time zone, and city list in the address, so anyone who opens it sees the same conversion shown in their browser.
