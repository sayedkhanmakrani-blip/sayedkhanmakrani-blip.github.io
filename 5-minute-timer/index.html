How to use it

Enter hours, minutes, and seconds, or tap a quick-set button, then press Start. The big display counts down and the browser tab title shows the remaining time, so you can keep an eye on it from another tab. Press Pause to hold the countdown and Resume to continue. When the time is up the screen flashes and, if “Sound alarm” is ticked, a short beep repeats for up to 30 seconds or until you press Start or Reset.

Keyboard shortcuts: Space starts or pauses, R resets. Full screen makes the digits fill your display, handy for classrooms, workouts, meetings, and cooking. You can also link to a preset length by adding ?t= and a number of seconds to the address, for example ?t=90 for 90 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Will the timer keep running in a background tab?

Yes. It counts against the real clock, so the time stays correct even if your browser slows down background tabs. The alarm sound may be delayed in some browsers when the tab is hidden.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page. Press Start yourself, make sure “Sound alarm” is ticked, and check your device volume.

Does it remember my last time?

Yes. Your last countdown length is saved in your browser’s local storage on this device only.
