BMI Calculator

Calculate your Body Mass Index in metric or imperial units and see your WHO weight category.

How to use it

Choose metric or imperial units, then enter your weight and height. Your BMI appears instantly with its World Health Organization category. BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. For imperial units, the calculator converts pounds and feet/inches for you.

WHO adult categories: under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal weight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or more is obesity (class I 30–34.9, class II 35–39.9, class III 40+). BMI is a quick screening tool, not a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not designed for children, teens, or pregnant people. Talk with a healthcare professional about your individual health.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, the WHO considers 18.5 to 24.9 a normal weight range.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Muscular people can have a high BMI without excess body fat.

Can children use this calculator?

No. Children and teens need age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles.
