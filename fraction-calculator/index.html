Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply, and divide fractions and mixed numbers, with the answer simplified and the working shown.

Simplify a fraction or convert a decimal

How to use it

Type two numbers in any of these forms: a fraction (3/4), a mixed number with a space (2 1/3), a whole number (5), or a decimal (0.25). Pick an operation and the answer appears in lowest terms, as a mixed number when it is larger than one, and as a decimal. A short line of working shows how the result was reached.

To add or subtract, the fractions are put over a common denominator first. To multiply, the numerators and denominators are multiplied. To divide, the second fraction is flipped and then multiplied. Every result is reduced by dividing the top and bottom by their greatest common factor. The second box simplifies a single fraction or turns a decimal into a fraction, for example 0.375 = 3/8.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter a mixed number?

Put a space between the whole number and the fraction, for example 2 1/3.

Can it handle negative fractions?

Yes. Type a minus sign in front, such as -3/4 or -1 1/2.
