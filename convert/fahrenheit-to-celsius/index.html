How to convert fahrenheit to celsius

Use the formula °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Example: 68 °F works out to 20 °C. The two scales meet at −40, where −40 °C equals −40 °F. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F) at sea level.
