How to convert celsius to fahrenheit

Use the formula °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Example: 25 °C works out to 77 °F. The two scales meet at −40, where −40 °C equals −40 °F. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F) at sea level.
