Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months, and days, plus a countdown to your next birthday.

How to use it

Pick a date of birth. By default the calculator measures age as of today, but you can choose any "as of" date to find someone’s age on a past or future day, such as a school enrollment cutoff, a retirement date, or an anniversary. You will see age in completed years, months, and days, the total number of days lived, and how many days remain until the next birthday.

Months are counted by calendar month, so the result matches how people normally state age. People born on February 29 have their birthday counted on March 1 in non-leap years. All calculations happen in your browser using your local calendar.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

Full years are counted first, then remaining full months, then remaining days, borrowing days from the previous month when needed.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Yes. Set the optional as-of date to any day.

What about leap-day birthdays?

In non-leap years, a February 29 birthday is counted on March 1.
