Loan Calculator

Estimate your monthly loan payment, total interest, and full amortization schedule.

Amortization schedule

How to use it

Enter the amount you plan to borrow, the annual interest rate (APR) your lender quotes, and the loan term in years or months. The calculator updates instantly and shows your fixed monthly payment, the total you will repay, and how much of that is interest. Open the amortization schedule to see, month by month, how each payment splits between principal and interest and how your balance falls over time.

Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal. That is why making extra payments early can save a large amount of interest. Try shortening the term or lowering the rate to compare offers side by side. Results are estimates and do not include taxes, insurance, or fees.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?

It uses the standard amortization formula: payment = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments.

Does this include property tax or insurance?

No. The result covers principal and interest only. Add taxes, insurance, and HOA fees separately for a full housing cost.

Can I use it for car or personal loans?

Yes. Any fixed-rate loan with equal monthly payments works.
