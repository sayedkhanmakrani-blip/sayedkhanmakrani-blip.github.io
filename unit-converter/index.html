How to use it

Pick a category, type a value, and choose the units to convert from and to. The answer updates as you type, and the table underneath shows the same value in every other unit of that category, so you rarely need a second conversion. The Swap button flips the direction. The page address updates with your choices, so you can bookmark or share a specific conversion.

Conversion factors use exact international definitions where they exist: one inch is exactly 2.54 cm, one pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, and one US gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters. Results are rounded to 10 significant digits for display. Volume units marked “US” differ from UK (imperial) units; the UK gallon is listed separately.

Frequently asked questions

Are the conversions exact?

They use the exact defined factors (for example 1 in = 2.54 cm). Displayed results are rounded to 10 significant digits.

What is the difference between US and UK gallons?

A US gallon is 3.785 liters; a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters. Choose the one that matches your source.

Can I share a conversion?

Yes. The page address updates with your category, units, and value, so you can copy and share it.
