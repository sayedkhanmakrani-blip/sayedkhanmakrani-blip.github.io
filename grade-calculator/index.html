Grade Calculator

Find your weighted course grade from assignments and exams, and see the score you need on the final to reach your goal.

What do I need on the final?

How to use it

Enter each graded item (homework, quizzes, exams) with your percentage score and the share of the course grade it is worth. The calculator multiplies each score by its weight and divides by the total weight entered so far, so it works even before the course is finished. The letter grade uses a common US scale (93 and up is an A, 90–92.99 is A-, and so on); your teacher’s cut-offs may differ.

The final exam section fills in your current grade automatically. Enter the grade you want and how much the final counts, and it solves for the score you need: needed = (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight.

Frequently asked questions

What if my weights don’t add up to 100%?

The average is taken over the weights you entered, which shows your grade so far.

How is the needed final score calculated?

Needed = (target − current × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight, with weights as decimals.
