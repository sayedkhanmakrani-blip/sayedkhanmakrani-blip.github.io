Image Compressor

Shrink JPEG, PNG, and WebP images in your browser. Pick a quality, compare sizes, and download. Nothing is uploaded.

JPEG has no transparency; transparent areas become white. Re-encoding removes photo metadata such as EXIF location.

How to use it

Choose or drop one or more JPEG, PNG, or WebP images. Each image is drawn onto an HTML canvas in your browser and saved again in the format and quality you pick, so nothing is uploaded and there is no file limit beyond your device’s memory. You see the old and new dimensions and file sizes side by side, plus how much smaller the new file is. Change any setting and the images are processed again.

For photos, JPEG or WebP at quality 60–80 usually gives a large saving with little visible difference. PNG output is lossless, so it only gets smaller if you also reduce the dimensions; if a result is larger than your original, keep the original. Setting a maximum width or height shrinks images proportionally and never enlarges them. Download files one by one or with Download all.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression and resizing happen in your browser using the Canvas API. The files never leave your device.

Why did my PNG get bigger?

Browsers save PNG losslessly without advanced optimization. Choose WebP or JPEG, or reduce the dimensions, for a smaller file.

Is image metadata kept?

No. Re-encoding drops EXIF data such as camera details and GPS location.
