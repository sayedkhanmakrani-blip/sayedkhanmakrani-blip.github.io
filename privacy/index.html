Privacy Policy

All calculations on this site run locally in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored, or shared.

Some tools remember your settings (for example your last timer length, your time zone list, or your GPA courses) using your browser’s local storage. That data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Images you compress or resize are processed on your device and never uploaded.

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. Like any website, the hosting server may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested). If advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated first.
