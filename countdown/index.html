    How to use it

    Type a title, pick the date and time, and press Start countdown. The display shows the days, hours, minutes, and seconds left and refreshes several times a second against your device clock, so it stays correct in a background tab. The browser tab title shows the time remaining too.

    The page address updates with your event, so you can bookmark it or press Copy link to share it. The time is read in the viewer’s own time zone, so a countdown to midnight reaches zero at local midnight for everyone. Press Save on this device to keep a list of countdowns in your browser’s local storage; nothing is uploaded.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I share my countdown?

    Yes. The link contains the title, date, and time, so anyone who opens it sees the same countdown.

    Which time zone does it use?

    The date and time are interpreted in the local time zone of whoever opens the page.

    Where are saved countdowns stored?

    In your browser’s local storage on this device only. Clearing your browser data removes them.
