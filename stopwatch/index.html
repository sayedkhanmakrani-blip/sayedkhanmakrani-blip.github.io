Stopwatch

A free online stopwatch with lap times, keyboard shortcuts, and full-screen mode, accurate to 1/100 of a second.

00:00.00

How to use it

Press Start to begin timing and Stop to pause; press Resume to continue from where you left off. While it is running, Lap records the time for the current lap and the running total; the newest lap appears at the top of the table. Reset clears the time and all laps.

Keyboard shortcuts: Space starts and stops, L records a lap, R resets. The display shows hundredths of a second and is based on your device clock, so it stays accurate even if the tab is in the background. Laps are not saved after you leave the page.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

It reads your device clock, so elapsed time is accurate; the display refreshes about 30 times per second.

Are my lap times saved?

No. They are kept on the page only and cleared when you reset or close it.
