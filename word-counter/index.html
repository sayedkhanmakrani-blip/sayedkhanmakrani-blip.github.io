Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs, estimate reading time, and change text case.

How to use it

Type or paste text into the box and the counts update instantly: words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and an estimated reading time based on an average adult silent reading speed of about 238 words per minute. It is handy for essays with word limits, social posts with character limits, meta descriptions, and cover letters.

Use the case buttons to convert your text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or Sentence case, then press Copy to put it on your clipboard. Paragraphs are separated by blank lines. Your text never leaves your device; nothing is saved or uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Any run of characters separated by spaces or line breaks counts as one word.

How is reading time estimated?

Word count divided by roughly 238 words per minute, rounded up.

Is my text stored?

No. Everything runs in your browser and is never uploaded.
