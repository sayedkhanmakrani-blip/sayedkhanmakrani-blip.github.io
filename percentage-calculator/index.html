How to use it

Type numbers into any of the three calculators; answers appear as you type. Use the first to find a portion, such as a 15% tip on a $48 bill (15% of 48 = 7.20). Use the second to express one number as a share of another, such as a test score of 42 out of 50 (84%). Use the third to measure growth or decline: a price moving from $80 to $100 is a 25% increase, while $100 to $80 is a 20% decrease.

Notice that percent change depends on the starting value, which is why increases and decreases between the same two numbers differ. Percent change from zero is undefined, so the calculator will tell you when that happens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30.

How is percent change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Why is a 50% drop not undone by a 50% gain?

Because the gain is measured from a smaller base. 100 falling 50% gives 50; a 50% gain on 50 only returns to 75.
